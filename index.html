Most portfolio reflections sound like this

“I learned a lot this semester.”

Zwiers calls this kind of turn a one-liner: true, polite, and finished before it starts. Nothing gets built, so nothing sticks.

“Conversation was not invented to help people choose right answers on tests.”

Chapter 2: Building Ideas

Ideas get built one turn at a time.

Zwiers asks students to see themselves as idea builders. Partners take an idea and make it stronger with two moves: clarifying what it means and supporting it with evidence.

Chapter 3: Collaborative Arguments

Argue to decide, not to win.

In a collaborative argument, partners build up every side before choosing. Then they weigh the evidence. Honest reflection works the same way: build the case for your growth and the case against it.
